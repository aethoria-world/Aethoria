    Chapter I

    Born of two worlds, yet accepted by neither, Dragon Knight is a half-blood child of the Skyrith and humankind.

    For his mixed heritage, his wings were severed in childhood, stripping him of the birthright of his mother's people. His mother was condemned to death, while the identity of his human father remains shrouded in mystery.

    Left to perish, he was saved by a small band of goblins, who became his unlikely companions.

    Yet fate had other plans for the outcast. Through courage, wit, and an extraordinary gift, he eventually forged a bond with a dragon - a creature few could ever hope to command.

    Now, astride his mighty companion, he wanders the vast lands of Aethoria, a lone traveler beneath the endless skies, belonging to no kingdom and bound by no borders.
