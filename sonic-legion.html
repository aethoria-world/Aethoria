    Chapter I

    The Sonic Legion is the elite military force of the Skyrith, a proud and ancient race of winged beings who dwell among the highest peaks of Aethoria.

    Renowned for their extraordinary speed, aerial mastery, and unwavering discipline, the Legion commands the skies with unmatched precision and devastating force. Its warriors are among the finest of the Skyrith, bound by honor, loyalty, and an unshakable sense of superiority.

    To behold the Sonic Legion descending from the heavens is to witness the fury of the skies unleashed.
